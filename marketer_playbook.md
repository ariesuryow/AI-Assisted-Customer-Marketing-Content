# Marketer Playbook: Using the AI Content Generator

**Who this is for:** marketing and content team members, no coding needed.

## 1. Before you start
- Pick the **segment** (who), **channel** (where) and **seasonal angle** (when).
- Check the segment's **approved offers**. The AI can only use these. Need a new offer? Add it to the list first.

## 2. What you can and cannot put into AI tools
| OK | Not OK |
|---|---|
| Segment descriptions ("recent, high-spend customers") | Customer names, emails, phone numbers, Customer IDs |
| Product categories and themes | Individual order history |
| Approved offers and brand voice | Confidential pricing or unreleased campaign plans |

## 3. Review checklist (every item, every time)
- [ ] Matches the segment and its goal
- [ ] Offers are real and approved, with no invented discount, deadline or free gift
- [ ] No pressure or false urgency
- [ ] One clear call to action
- [ ] Right length and tone for the channel
- [ ] Facts about products and brand are correct
- [ ] Reads like us, not like a generic AI template

Items marked in the `auto_flags` column need extra attention, but items without flags still need review.

## 4. Edit, score, decide
Fill in the 1-5 scores, set `human_decision` to **approve**, **edit** or **reject**, and add a note if you changed something. The notes help improve the prompts.

## 5. Escalate when
- The content mentions a price, discount value or deadline
- It touches legal, health, or sensitive topics
- The same issue appears repeatedly (the prompt needs fixing)
- You are unsure whether data is allowed in the tool

Ask the data/AI owner or your manager before publishing.

## 6. Remember
AI drafts, **people decide**. Nothing goes out unreviewed.
